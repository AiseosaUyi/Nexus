# Arc — playbook (kind: inbound)

Remote-first talent network (Arc.dev), vetted but lighter than Toptal. Leans engineering-heavy,
which suits the design-engineer positioning better than a pure-design framing would.

Rated 4/5 region-friendly, medium difficulty, high potential.

## Setup (one-off — Aise does the signup)
- Position as **design engineer / front-end**, not "product designer." Arc's demand is weighted
  toward engineering roles; the design side is the differentiator on top, not the headline.
- Stack to surface: React, Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase.
- Nexus is the strongest single artifact here — a product designed *and* built end to end, in
  production. Lead with it.

## Read (each run)
- Job matches, recruiter messages, application statuses.

## Draft
- Applications: engineering-audience tone. Concrete stack, what you owned, what shipped.
  Design credibility is the closer, not the opener.
- Capture as `type=job`.

## Health inputs
- Vetting status, profile completeness, stack tags, application→reply rate.

## Execute (after approval)
- Open role → Apply → paste approved text → submit → `cc_mark_sent`.
