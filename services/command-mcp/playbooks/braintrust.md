# Braintrust — playbook (kind: inbound)

Talent network with a real approval gate, but a light one — no technical assessment. Enterprise
clients. Talent sets their own rate and **keeps 100% of it**; Braintrust adds 15% on top of the
client bill instead of taking a cut.

Rated 4/5 region-friendly, medium difficulty, very high potential.

**Nigeria note:** Braintrust operates in 100+ countries and partnered with Stripe to pay talent
in **USDC** specifically for countries where local banking is hard. That makes it more viable
from Nigeria than the 4-star rating implies — payout is usually the blocker on platforms like
this, and here it is solved.

## Setup (one-off — Aise does all of this personally)
Approval sequence, in order:
1. Profile: name, why you're joining, interests (pick **Design** and **Web3**).
2. Skills: select **at least 5**, then nominate **top 3 as "Superpowers."**
   Recommended superpowers: `Product Design`, `Design Systems`, `Front-end Development`.
3. Résumé — the stated approval criterion is a detailed résumé matching current client needs.
   Use the same proof points as Contra: 1.5M users at Game7, $10.4M seed at Bento, $1.5M at Valist.
4. **Identity verification** — government ID plus a selfie. Aise only; never assisted.
5. **AIR screening interview** — 5–10 minutes with their AI interviewer. Not technical. It asks
   about previous work experience. Prep: three stories with numbers in them, told in ~90 seconds each.

Review lands within 24h, up to 72h over a weekend. Approved profiles get a vetted badge and
only then do applications become visible to clients.

## Read (each run)
- Job feed filtered to design / design-engineering, remote.
- Proposal statuses and client messages.

## Draft
- Proposals: enterprise buyers, so lead with risk reduction and specifics, not enthusiasm.
  Name the outcome, the stack, the timeline.
- Capture as `type=job`.

## Health inputs
- Approval status first — until approved, health is capped low regardless of profile quality.
- Then: superpowers set, résumé attached, proposal→reply rate.

## Execute (after approval)
- Open role → Apply → paste approved proposal → submit → `cc_mark_sent`.
- Rates and offers are Aise's call, never the operator's.
