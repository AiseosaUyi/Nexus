# Toptal — playbook (kind: inbound)

Highest ceiling on the list and the hardest gate. Accepts **under 3%** of applicants.

Rated 3/5 region-friendly, hard, extremely high potential.

## ⚠️ Do not apply casually — rejection costs 6 months

Rejection at **any** stage means a **6-month wait** before reapplying. This single fact should
drive the sequencing: Toptal is the *last* platform to attempt, not the first. Apply only once
the Contra portfolio is deep (multi-image case studies, not single hero shots) and there are
recommendations on file. A thin application now burns the option until roughly January.

Treat "not yet" as the default answer until the prep checklist below is genuinely complete.

## The five stages (2–4 weeks end to end)
1. **English communication screening** — live/video with a screener. Clients are US and European
   paying premium rates; they are testing client-call fluency and confidence, not grammar.
2. **Portfolio review + short design challenge.**
3. **Live problem-solving** with a screener. This is the hardest stage and reportedly cuts ~70%
   of whoever is left.
4. **Test project** — multi-week, and paid.
5. Final review.

## Prep checklist (all must be true before applying)
- [ ] 3+ case studies showing *process*, not just outcome: problem → constraints → decisions → result.
- [ ] Each one tells a decision story out loud in ~3 minutes without notes.
- [ ] Live design exercise rehearsed — narrating reasoning while designing is a distinct skill
      from designing, and stage 3 tests the narration.
- [ ] Contra recommendations landed (external credibility for the portfolio review).
- [ ] A clear specialism stated. "Product designer who ships front-end" beats "generalist."

## Read (each run — only once accepted)
- Job matches from Toptal's matcher, and messages from talent matchers.

## Draft
- Toptal is matcher-mediated: the matcher's opinion of you drives what you see. Replies to
  matchers are as important as client-facing text. Prompt, specific, no fluff.

## Health inputs
- Pre-application: prep checklist completion, not profile completeness.
- Post-acceptance: matcher response time, match→interview rate.

## Execute (after approval)
- Never start or schedule a screening stage on Aise's behalf. Interviews are his to book and sit.
