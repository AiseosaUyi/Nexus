# Flexiple — playbook (kind: inbound)

Small, hand-vetted network. Low volume, higher-quality matches. Human screening call rather
than an automated funnel.

Rated 3/5 region-friendly, hard, high potential.

## Sequencing
Lowest priority of the five new platforms. Hard gate, modest volume. Worth doing *after*
Wellfound and Braintrust are live and producing, not before — the effort/return ratio only
makes sense once the easier platforms are saturated.

## Setup (one-off — Aise does the signup)
- Application is short but the screening call is human and conversational. Same three stories
  with numbers as Braintrust.
- Specialism matters more than breadth here given the small client pool.

## Read (each run)
- Email and dashboard — Flexiple is lower-frequency than the marketplaces. Weekly is enough;
  daily checks are wasted effort.

## Draft
- Matches are human-mediated. Replies should read like talking to a colleague, not a form.

## Health inputs
- Vetting status, then match→interview rate. Volume is low, so do not read a quiet week as a
  problem to fix.

## Execute (after approval)
- Never book or sit the screening call on Aise's behalf.
